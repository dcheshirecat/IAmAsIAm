## Park

> **Objective:** Join the morning practice

**Explore** (glowing things; the player picks):

- **Look: The pavilion**
    - The red pavilion in the corner, and under it, twenty people in loose clothes moving very slowly, all together.
- **Look: Lin Daozhang**
    - In the back row, in an old windbreaker, moving like water finding its way down a hill.
- **Use: The back row (join in)**

**Lin Daozhang:** Don't copy the shapes. Copy the slowness. Feet first. Knees soft. Let the arms come along later.

**Explore** (glowing things; the player picks):

- **Use: Your feet (soft knees)**
    - Weight into one foot. Then, slowly, the other. Your arms float up without being sent. You are in your body the whole time.
- **Wait (watch, then try one move)**
    - One move. Push the water away. Bring the water back. Lin Daozhang nods: one is enough today.

**Maya:** In Nepal I sat still for ten days. I lost myself completely. This is the first time being still didn't scare me.

**Lin Daozhang:** Sitting still with force is still force. Water doesn't try. It goes around the rock, and in a thousand years the rock is gone.

## Evening

> **Objective:** Bring Auntie Mei's container back

**Explore** (glowing things; the player picks):

- **Talk: Lin Daozhang**

**Lin Daozhang:** Six generations of priests in Tainan, my family. My father taught me the rites when I was twelve. I hated it. I wanted to play baseball.

**Lin Daozhang:** Then I came here, for my wife's job, and the rain. Nobody here needed a priest. Then everybody did. So: taxes on Monday, the gods on Sunday.

**Explore** (glowing things; the player picks):

- **Talk: Lin Daozhang (ask if he still wanted to be a priest)**

- **Talk: Lin Daozhang (ask about baseball)**
    - **Maya:** Do you still like baseball?
    - **Lin Daozhang:** Every Mariners game. They lose, I practise acceptance. Very spiritual team.
