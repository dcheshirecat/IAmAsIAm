# Ideas for chapters after Buddhism and Islam

**Status (2026-11-11):** the owner chose Hinduism, Sikhism, Daoism, Druidry, Humanism, Unitarian Universalism, Jainism,
Hellenic polytheism and Eastern Orthodox Christianity (chapters 5–13 in `chapters.json`). The new four are now researched in
`traditions.json`, with practitioner testimony still to gather.

Order so far: Prologue Quaker → Prelude Wicca → 1 Christianity → 2 Judaism → 3 Buddhism → 4 Islam → ?

"Open" here means outsiders are welcome to visit, learn and take part in at least some of the practice. Each one has
many streams, and the chapters show that diversity (owner, 2026-11-11).

## Already researched (in traditions.json)
| Tradition | Openness | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Hinduism | welcome to learn and visit | Temples (mandirs) welcome visitors. Streams: Vaishnava, Shaiva, Shakta, Smarta, and ISKCON (which actively welcomes converts) |
| Sikhism | welcome to learn and visit | Every gurdwara is open to all, with a free communal meal (langar). Head covering inside |
| Daoism | welcome to learn and visit | Temples, tai chi and qigong, the Daodejing. Monastic (Quanzhen) and householder (Zhengyi) streams |
| Shinto | welcome to learn and visit | Shrine visits, omamori charms and omikuji fortunes are open to everyone |
| Bahá'í Faith | open to all | Devotional gatherings and study circles open to anyone. No clergy |
| Druidry | open to all | Open orders (e.g. OBOD), seasonal rituals, nature-based. Close to Wicca |
| Heathenry | open to all (inclusive groups only) | Norse/Germanic. Only inclusive groups are shown, since some "folkish" groups are racially exclusive |
| Humanism | open to all | The non-religious path, always available |

## Not playable (closed or initiatory)
Indigenous traditions and the African diasporic traditions that require initiation (e.g. Vodou, Santería/Lukumí,
Candomblé) are shown only with respect, as closed. Nothing from them is played.
