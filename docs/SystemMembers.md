# The system: names and roles (DRAFT for the owner to correct)

**Status:** suggestions only. Correct the names and roles, add or remove members, and fill in **Relationship with faith**.
Once confirmed, Claude puts them in `Resources/Data/system_members.json`. The earlier draft cast (Wren, Juniper, Pip,
Bramble, Sorrel) is retired.

The backstory these roles respond to (owner, 2026-11-11): raised by a **single mother, a Quaker minister, who neglected
raising her child in favour of the congregation**. The character later left organised religion and believes in Wicca.

The role names are the ones commonly used in plural and OSDD communities. They describe what a part does, not who they are.
